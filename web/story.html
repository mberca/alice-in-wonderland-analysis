    06

    A few ground rules.

    There’s plenty of room to be curious. These are the rules that keep the counting honest while we’re exploring.

    One pinned edition.

    Millennium Fulcrum Edition 3.0, supplied as a local Gutenberg file. The original bytes and SHA-256 are retained. Narrative analysis excludes the title page, chapter headings, “THE END”, end credits, and legal boilerplate.

    A word, explicitly defined.

    Unicode letters with internal apostrophes and hyphens. Case-folding is optional; final ’s grouping is separate. No stemming, entity resolution, dialogue attribution, or sentiment model is hidden behind the charts.

    Descriptions, not discoveries of cause.

    These are exact counts under stated rules in one edition, not estimates of a literary population. No causal, significance, emotion, authorship, or character-importance claims follow from them.

    Evidence is computed, not generated.

    The Python standard library computes every figure and export. No API keys, paid models, cloud cluster, external font, or CDN are needed. The browser keeps its search data on the page; queries never leave your device.
